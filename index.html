What this board can establish
This dashboard does not reconstruct hidden referee state and does not estimate missing balances.
Published
What the referee says
Room payloads, PnL scores, listed positions, aggregates, omissions, sweep numbers and hashes. “Published” identifies the source; it does not claim a full ledger audit.
Derived
What we calculate
Groups of equal decimal scores, provisional visible place spans, countdowns, record age and imbalance in counts of long/short owners. Rounded equal scores need not be exact underlying ties.
Unknown
What stays unknown
Unlisted DIDs, complete balances and ranks, full cutoff tie populations and final individual prizes. A hash alone does not provide the underlying data.
Reading the health indicators
LIVE means a usable record posted within 12 minutes (an interface threshold, not a contest rule). STALE means older data or an implausible future timestamp. PARTIAL means a missing/failed room, skipped malformed records, inconsistent sweeps/hashes, unverified signatures, or disclosed flow omissions. ERROR means no usable record. A fresh Top feed is still incomplete.
Five small JSON reads every 60 seconds while this tab is visible; manual refresh is throttled to the same interval. The most recent five room messages are requested. Up to 200 observed sweeps per room are retained in memory for search during this session. Search is case-sensitive because DIDs are case-sensitive, and historical sightings never establish current status.
Envelope signatures are checked locally with the public referee DID observed during the audit. A successful signature check establishes consistency with that DID; it does not independently authenticate the launch, reconstruct the state root, or verify archived files. Browsers without Ed25519 support show signature verification unavailable.
